## Subsequent clarification for the website update

The observer explains that the four heads under the initial, deepest square were first trimmed with the compact excavator's breaker. Concrete was leveled around them flush with their tops, leaving their bars projecting. Shuttering enclosed the square, white plastic covered the base and sides except at exposed shaft heads, heavy reinforcement was installed, and a succession of concrete trucks supplied the final reinforced block pour. The base placement and block pour are distinct. This same process is repeating in three adjoining, shallower rectangles, some with four shafts and some two; reinforcement is just beginning in the newer areas. Two cranes are now helping from opposite ends to increase the pace, according to the observer. The October 13–21 photo sequence supports the progression; exact structural design and sheet specification remain unconfirmed.
