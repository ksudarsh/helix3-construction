# iPhone 12 high-resolution sequence review

Eighteen HEIC camera files from `images/iPhone12/` in the supplied folder were reviewed. All are preserved unchanged in `images/originals/`; hashes, dimensions, local filesystem modification times and selections are in `2026-10-07-to-09-iphone12-heic.json`. Their browser JPEG conversions were made with the bundled `heif-convert`.

Fourteen files (IMG_0149–0151, 0153, 0155–0162, 0165–0166) are the same frames as lower-resolution JPEGs already on the site. A 128-pixel comparison produced mean squared errors of 0.4–0.8 RGB units after resizing. These are photographic duplicates, but the HEICs retain 4032 × 2268 or 2268 × 4032 camera resolution versus the published 1536 × 864 or 864 × 1536 JPEGs. The gallery continues to show the established JPEG viewing copies and now offers the high-resolution HEIC originals for download.

Four frames had no earlier site equivalent. IMG_0154 is a focused October 7 Silvi mixer view that adds no distinct construction stage beyond the published concrete-supply photographs. IMG_0167 provides the selected wider midday October 9 view of a white sheet across much of the formed inner square. IMG_0168 is a closer overlapping view of the same operation. IMG_0169 is selected for the close view of the sheet, projecting bars and worker handling material at the right edge. All four HEICs are archived; IMG_0167 and IMG_0169 have browser JPEGs, self-contained annotated SVGs, journal entries and gallery entries. IMG_0154 and IMG_0168 are intentionally omitted from the visual journal as overlapping coverage.

The corrected observable sequence is: deepest inner square excavated and exposed → concrete placed and finished October 7 → shuttering delivered and erected October 8 → white sheet spread inside the forms by midday October 9 → sheet still present and bent rebar staged nearby October 11. The user's identification of the white material as plastic and expectation that the delivered rebar will enter the pit are recorded as observer identification and expectation, respectively. Material specification, function, precise relative timing of the rebar delivery and sheet installation, actual rebar installation, and final foundation design are not established.

Filesystem modification times indicate October 7–9 local dates and minute/second ordering for these files. The sequence also follows visible site changes; times have not been independently verified as camera EXIF capture timestamps.
